ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & EVALUATION GUIDELINES (2026-2027)

1. MID-SEMESTER AND END-SEMESTER EVALUATION
1.1 Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) carries 40% total weightage, comprising Mid-1 Examinations (15%), Mid-2 Examinations (15%), and Quizzes/Assignments (10%).
1.2 Mid-1 Examination syllabus covers Units 1, 2, and the first half of Unit 3. Mid-2 Examination covers the remaining half of Unit 3, Unit 4, and Unit 5.
1.3 Uncovered syllabus topics prior to scheduled mid-term examinations must be formally reported to the HOD at least 10 calendar days prior to exam start for syllabus moderation.

2. EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY & SYLLABUS MODERATION
2.1 If a course has completed less than 70% of total syllabus by the final week of instruction, the HOD and Academic Dean may order special weekend recovery workshops.
