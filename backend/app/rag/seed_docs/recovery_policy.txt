COURSE PROGRESS & ACADEMIC RECOVERY POLICY (2026-2027)

1. SYLLABUS PROGRESS MONITORING & DEFICIT THRESHOLDS
1.1 Weekly expected syllabus completion targets are established by the Academic Planning Committee prior to semester commencement.
1.2 Course progress risk levels are classified into four operational tiers:
    - ON TRACK: Actual coverage within 5% of expected target.
    - MINOR SLIPPAGE: Syllabus coverage gap between 5% and 12%. Course faculty must accelerate lecture pace by 1 topic per week.
    - SIGNIFICANT SLIPPAGE: Syllabus coverage gap between 12% and 25%. Faculty and Class Mentor must submit a 2-week catch-up schedule.
    - CRITICAL DEFICIT: Syllabus coverage gap exceeding 25% or predicted completion delay > 3 weeks.

2. MANDATORY RECOVERY ACTIONS FOR CRITICAL DEFICIT
2.1 When a course is flagged as CRITICAL DEFICIT, the Head of Department (HOD) is authorized to allocate up to 6 mandatory extra recovery lecture slots using open Friday afternoon and Saturday timetable slots.
2.2 The Recovery Engine optimizer must prioritize foundational prerequisite topics (e.g. Logic Resolution, Decision Trees, Virtual Memory Inodes) and streamline tutorial case studies.
2.3 Timetable recovery class allocations must not create slot conflicts for assigned faculty or student sections.
