OFFICIAL ATTENDANCE POLICY & GUIDELINES (2026-2027)

1. MANDATORY ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
1.1 Students must maintain a minimum aggregate attendance of 75% across all registered theoretical and laboratory courses in a semester to be eligible to appear for Semester End Examinations (SEE).
1.2 Condonation of attendance up to 10% (between 65% and 74%) may be granted by the Academic Council on medical grounds or official institutional representation, subject to submission of valid certificates and HOD recommendation.

2. FACULTY RECORDING RESPONSIBILITIES
2.1 Course faculty must log and verify lecture attendance within 24 hours of class completion via the Vignan Faculty Workstation.
2.2 If student attendance drops below 65% in any course at the mid-semester evaluation mark, automated alert notifications will be dispatched to parents, class mentors, and the Head of Department.

3. RECOVERY FOR SHORTAGE OF ATTENDANCE
3.1 Students with attendance between 60% and 74% due to verified extra-curricular or medical reasons must attend mandatory weekend remedial tutorial sessions to qualify for condonation approval.
